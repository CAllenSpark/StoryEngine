# Marketing Brand Bible — StoryEngine

> Sprint 1 seed. Owner: Design Leader. Consulted: PM.

## Name
**StoryEngine** (working title — placeholder; final name TBD).

## Tagline (drafts)
- *Tiny worlds. Whole stories. Five minutes at a time.*
- *A story a day, in the time it takes to finish a coffee.*
- *Hand-stitched adventures for in-between moments.*

## Positioning
Between mobile hyper-casual and story-heavy indies. Not a game you grind. Not a novel you schedule. A **five-minute ritual**: one adventure, one feeling, one clean finish.

## Voice
- Warm, curious, gently funny.
- Writes like a thoughtful friend, not a brand.
- Respects the player's time above all.
- No hype words. No FOMO. No dark patterns.

## Visual Identity (direction)
- Sprite-forward. Small pixel craft is the brand.
- Soft, hand-picked palettes per adventure — the logo lives comfortably on any of them.
- Generous whitespace. Nothing shouts.

## What We're Not
- Not a live-service game.
- Not ad-supported in V1.
- Not open-world.
- Not a platform for user-generated adventures (yet).

## Audience Proof Points
- "I finished a story on my lunch break."
- "I didn't know a game could move me in five minutes."
- "I actually finished this one."

## Launch Beats (sketch)
1. **Closed beta** — hand-picked story-game fans, one new adventure per week.
2. **Open beta** — public library, session-length dashboard shared publicly as proof of pillar.
3. **Mobile launch** — same catalog, native wrapper.

## Anti-Goals
- Retention metrics that reward bingeing over finishing.
- Any visual or copy that creates urgency or anxiety.
